    ? A quick guide to imageannot ⌄

    Coordinates that match your macros

    The top left is (0, 0); the bottom right is (1, 1). Both axes are normalized, and y increases downward. Zooming changes your view, never your coordinates.

    Draw, then paste

    Choose a fragment and drag to draw. For labels and markers, click their position. The result appears below the image and is copied automatically when your browser allows it.

    Cover and reveal

    Choose Whiteout to produce \highlightfrag[whiteout]: an opaque white rectangle. Its Reveal animation follows the definitions in your LaTeX project.

    Images and previews

    Use a direct image URL, drop a file, or share a link with ?image=IMAGE_URL. Local files stay in your browser. Typography and TikZ previews are approximate; the exported command uses your original macros.
